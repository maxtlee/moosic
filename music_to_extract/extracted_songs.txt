Songs extracted from screenshots in music_to_extract
====================================================
Updated 2026-08-19.

Method
------
- Local Tesseract OCR (eng + chi_sim) on every screenshot, after cropping
  the song list, upscaling, and (for dark UI) inverting contrast.
- Screenshots are 1920x1080, often at 40–60% browser zoom, so titles were
  double-checked by hand and against the public playlist pages whose URLs
  appear in the address bars.
- Apple Music was rebuilt as the union of:
    (1) a handwritten library transcript (typos corrected),
    (2) Chinese titles from close-up screenshots in music_to_extract/newss/,
    (3) the earlier Aug 6 library screenshots, for songs that appear there
        but not in the later handwritten list.
- YouTube Music and public Spotify playlists were confirmed against the live
  playlist pages (same IDs as in the screenshots). Spotify Liked Songs is
  private, so that section is OCR + manual reading only.
